THE CASE-STUDY METHOD

Every project should answer four questions.

01

What was the problem?

Define the operational or commercial friction without hiding behind jargon.

02

What changed?

Explain the process, system, control or intervention that was introduced.

03

What was the scale?

Use operating volumes, functions, plants, containers, movements or other supported evidence.

04

What was the impact?

Show a measurable outcome where the underlying evidence supports the number—and label stated outcomes clearly.
