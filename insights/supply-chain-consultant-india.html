THE PRACTICAL VIEW

Supply chain is not just logistics.

A supply chain consultant looks across material, money, movement and information. The objective is to identify constraints, quantify their effect, design controls and make better execution repeatable.

MATERIAL

Procurement & inventory

Supplier coordination, material availability, stores, inventory controls and sourcing decisions.

MOVEMENT

Logistics & dispatch

Transport planning, high-volume dispatch, port interfaces, liquid cargo and physical flow.

MONEY

Commercial control

Documentation, reconciliation, GST, Customs, cost controls and finance interfaces.

INFORMATION

Data & automation

MIS, Excel automation, analytics and visibility that support faster decisions.

A PRACTICAL METHOD

Six steps from problem to control.

01

Map the flow

Understand how material, money, movement and information actually travel through the operation.

02

Locate the constraint

Separate the visible symptom from the process constraint causing it.

03

Quantify

Use operational and commercial data to establish scale and priority.

04

Design the control

Create a practical process, ownership model or management control.

05

Automate repeatable work

Use structured data, SAP, Excel, MIS or other appropriate tools where automation genuinely helps.

06

Measure

Track whether the change improves visibility, reliability, cost, cycle time or control.
