HOW I THINK

A repeatable method for operational problems.

01 · MAP

Understand the flow

Map material → money → movement → information before changing the process.

02 · FIND

Locate the constraint

Identify where the process loses time, accuracy, visibility or commercial control.

03 · QUANTIFY

Put a number on it

Translate the issue into cost, time, inventory, errors, compliance exposure or working impact where the evidence supports it.

04 · CONTROL

Design the intervention

Clarify process, ownership, data and controls so execution becomes more reliable.

05 · AUTOMATE

Remove repeatable manual work

Use Excel, SAP, MIS and structured workflows where automation can improve speed and accuracy.

06 · MEASURE

Make the outcome visible

Compare the process before and after, then turn the result into a repeatable operating standard.
